Our Story

Built by founders, for founders.

We started SaVi Tech because we believed great digital products should come from people who actually care — not from agency layers and account managers.

SaVi Technologies was founded with a straightforward goal: build digital flagships, online stores, and business automations that deliver measurable commercial value without unnecessary agency overhead.

The studio was co-founded by Sahera Banu (Creative Direction & Brand Strategy) and Vijay Prasad (Product Architecture & Engineering Systems). Together with our specialized engineering and automation associates, we build resilient, high-speed digital products for modern brands.

From boutique brand flagships to full-scale e-commerce stores with automated Pan-India fulfillment, we focus on what matters: clean aesthetics, sub-second performance, and real business results.

Founded on direct founder accountability, senior craft, and transparent delivery.
